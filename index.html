What exactly does Atomiqs do — in one sentence?
Core value prop

We build a live digital twin of your enterprise processes — not the version documented in Visio or SharePoint, but what is actually happening across your systems, teams, and workflows — and we keep it continuously current so every programme, AI initiative, and change decision starts from reality, not guesswork.

I already use Claude, ChatGPT, or Copilot. Can't I just upload my process docs and get the same thing?
vs General AI

LLMs are exceptional at reasoning over content you give them. The problem is what you give them. Uploading documents gives the AI your designed process — not the one actually running. The gap between those two is exactly where cost, risk, and failed initiatives live.

Atomiqs doesn't read static documents — it actively discovers what is happening across your live systems, structures it into a continuously updated intelligence graph, and makes that context available to your AI tools. So Claude, Copilot, or any agent you deploy gets grounded, governed business understanding — not a stale file snapshot.

We run SAP and they're building a Business Data Cloud with a knowledge layer. Do we still need Atomiqs?
vs SAP BDC

SAP's Business Data Cloud is a genuinely promising direction — and we welcome it. But it is still evolving, and by design it covers SAP's own ecosystem. The average enterprise runs 40–60+ systems. SAP BDC won't cover your non-SAP workflows, legacy tools, Teams conversations, or the end-to-end process that cuts across all of them.

If you prefer to anchor on SAP BDC, that works well — we connect via A2A communication so SAP handles its own context and work execution, while Atomiqs covers the rest of your landscape and delivers last-mile process intelligence SAP cannot reach. SAP goes deep inside its ecosystem. Atomiqs covers the full enterprise picture.

We already have a process mining tool like Celonis or Signavio. Isn't that enough?
vs Process mining

Process mining excels at surfacing how event-log data flows and where value leaks — but the output is a dashboard or report. It rarely becomes an owned, governed, actionable asset the wider business can work from. Insights stay with analysts and stop at a slide.

Atomiqs takes mined output further — elevating it into a living process model with ownership, SOPs, benchmarks, and change impact attached, then activating it directly into your programmes and AI tooling. We extend what you already have rather than replace it.

We don't use an ERP — our operations are largely manual or spreadsheet-driven. Is Atomiqs still relevant for us?
Manual & non-ERP environments

Yes — and arguably this is where the value is highest. Manual and spreadsheet-heavy operations are where process knowledge is most invisible and most at risk. It lives in people's heads, in email chains, in tabs nobody else can read. When those people leave, the knowledge leaves with them.

Atomiqs captures how work actually runs regardless of the tools underneath — whether that's a formal ERP, a set of Excel files, Teams channels, or a mix of all three. We surface the process, structure it, assign ownership, and make it something your organisation can govern, improve, and hand off — without needing to replace a single tool you currently use.
